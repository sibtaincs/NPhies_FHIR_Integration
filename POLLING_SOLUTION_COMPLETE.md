? THE QUESTION
????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????

"How will I record polling data table not created?"

Translation: "How do I record/store polling request/response data
   when there's no dedicated table for it?"


????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????
? THE SOLUTION
????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????????

Created a dedicated PollingRecord table!

This table stores:
  ? Every polling request sent to NPHIES
  ? Every polling response received
  ? Complete audit trail (timestamps, status changes)
  ? Error tracking and retry management
  ? Performance metrics (duration, response times)
  ? Full FHIR bundles for debugging
  ? Provider and message type tracking
