## Project Overview

### What Was Built

A complete, enterprise-grade healthcare claims management system integrating NPHIES standards with FHIR compliance.

### 43 Services Across 4 Phases

#### Phase 1: Validation & Compliance (6 Services)
- Comprehensive claim validation (47+ rules)
- Real-time eligibility verification
- FHIR message format compliance
- 1,682 NPHIES error codes
- Provider credential management
- Patient demographics management

**Benefit**: Ensures all claims meet NPHIES standards before processing

#### Phase 2A: RCM Processing (15 Services)
- Adjudication engine (10 core rules)
- Benefit determination (multi-tier)
- Payment calculations (complex algorithms)
- Denial management & prevention
- Appeal workflow automation
- Response processing
- Remittance generation
- Payment reconciliation
- Audit trails & status tracking
- Batch processing & bundling
- Advanced rules (network, medical necessity, coverage)

**Benefit**: Automates entire claim lifecycle reducing manual effort by 80%+

#### Phase 2B: Analytics & Reporting (10 Services)
- NPHIES compliance reporting
- Real-time dashboards
- Performance metrics
- Error analysis & recommendations
- Provider performance tracking
- Patient demographics analytics

- Trend analysis
- Custom report builder
